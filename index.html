New Era Protect Ingredient Details

Crataeva Nurvala

Crataeva nurvala is a botanical traditionally used in Ayurvedic wellness practices, particularly in formulas associated with urinary health. Its inclusion gives New Era Protect a plant-based component specifically relevant to the product's urinary-wellness positioning.

Horsetail Extract

Horsetail, also known as Equisetum arvense, is an established botanical used in traditional herbal preparations. It contains naturally occurring plant compounds and is commonly found in nutritional formulas focused on general structural and urinary wellness.

Resveratrol

Resveratrol is a naturally occurring polyphenol. New Era Protect uses Japanese knotweed as its listed source of resveratrol. This compound is widely researched for its antioxidant properties and cellular-health relevance.

Boswellia Serrata

Boswellia serrata is a botanical traditionally used in wellness practices and extensively discussed in research concerning inflammatory pathways. Within a supplement formula, it provides an additional plant-based component for general wellness support.

Sumac

Sumac is a plant source containing naturally occurring compounds with antioxidant characteristics. Its presence adds another botanical component to the New Era Protect formula.

Hops Extract

Perluxan hops extract is included as part of the product's botanical blend. Hops has a long history of use in traditional wellness and is commonly associated with relaxation and general comfort.

Vitamin D3

Vitamin D3, or cholecalciferol, is an essential nutrient that contributes to normal muscle function and helps maintain normal bones. Its inclusion provides nutritional support beyond the botanical ingredients.

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral involved in numerous normal biological processes. It contributes to normal immune function and supports normal cellular processes throughout the body.

The role of an individual ingredient should not be interpreted as proof that New Era Protect can prevent, diagnose, or treat a medical condition. Ingredient research and finished-product evidence are not the same thing.

When Urinary Symptoms Need Professional Attention

It is important not to assume that every urinary concern can be addressed with a dietary supplement. Changes in urinary function can have many possible causes, and identifying the cause may require professional evaluation.

Seek appropriate medical attention for symptoms such as painful urination, blood in the urine, fever, significant pelvic or back pain, sudden changes in urinary function, or symptoms that persist or become worse.

New Era Protect can be considered as a nutritional wellness product, but it should never delay appropriate medical care when symptoms are concerning or unexplained.
